Route

Goal, risk, assets, budget, limits. The frame the agent cannot step outside.

Observe

Prices, volatility and drawdowns — plus your spending, for round-ups.

Decide

Execute

Routes through a DEX, settles from your vault. One buy, one waybot.

Guardrails are in the contract, not in trust

Allow-listed assets only. A hard spend cap. No transfers to external addresses. A fully compromised agent still cannot move your money or buy something off the list — withdrawal keys never leave you.
